# G17 — Dense production-art library

_Status: Backlog only. This is future scope, not implementation authority._

## Sequence and gate

G17 is queued after completion and review of the already-scoped G01–G16
program. It must remain in Backlog until those predecessors are complete and
the maintainer explicitly instructs that G17 may start. The queue does not
authorize work on a garment, any other roadmap item, or any one-time or
recurring expense.

## Intended outcome

Build a dense, searchable, well-categorized artwork library selected for
fashion and garment-design use—not a generic image dump. Preserve clear
distinctions among source/reference photographs and studies, clean production
artwork, and repeat-verified tiles. Do not call a reference image
production-ready or a seamless repeat without evidence.

## Scope checkpoint before implementation

At the future start gate, refine and approve: garment/print use-cases; taxonomy
and category-density targets; accepted file formats and resolution; item-level
rights and provenance requirements; curation and duplicate policy; bundle-size
and load-performance limits; and the rendered-app verification matrix. Do not
invent numerical coverage or size targets before that checkpoint.

## Acceptance boundaries

- Every included asset has item-level source, provenance, rights/reuse evidence,
  and a clear presentation/use classification.
- The collection is relevant to garment and fashion-design workflows, dense
  enough for the approved use-cases, and findable with the approved categories,
  filters, and search.
- Reference photos/studies, clean artwork, and repeat-verified tiles are
  identified distinctly. Suitability guidance remains honest and does not
  silently upgrade evidence.
- Runtime remains local and makes no remote asset requests. Only no-cost,
  explicitly reusable assets may be acquired under the current cost hold; a
  paid license, service, storage plan, or other spend requires the maintainer
  to explicitly reopen that boundary before commitment.
- Verify actual rendered browsing, search/filter behavior, disclosure, narrow
  layout, local loading, and the approved bundle budget before closing G17.
