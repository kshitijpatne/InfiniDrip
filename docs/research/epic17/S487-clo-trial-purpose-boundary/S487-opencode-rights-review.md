## Codex correction

The raw report labels as an inference that this work is for a “commercial
product program.” The repository establishes product development and V03
receiver evaluation, but does not establish that the project or use is
commercial or for-profit. Codex does not adopt that classification. The
rights result is narrower: the terms exclude commercial/for-profit trial
purposes; the public evidence does not determine whether this exact project
falls inside that exclusion or establish a separate grant. Therefore it
cannot affirmatively pass the project's rights gate. This is a conservative
evidence decision, not a legal conclusion that use is prohibited or that any
past act breached a term.

OpenCode also reported subscription/billing mechanics. Those are outside
S487's scope and are not used for the decision.
