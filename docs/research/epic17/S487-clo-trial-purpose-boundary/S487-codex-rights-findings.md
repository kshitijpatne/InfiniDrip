# S487 — CLO trial-purpose rights findings

## Question and result

Can the currently published CLO terms affirmatively clear V03 Gate 0 for
future G04 product-development exchange tests using the owner-reported
14-day CLO trial?

**Result: no. Keep CLO outside Gate 0 for this product-development use unless
the applicable rights are later established.** This is an evidence-gate
decision, not a legal opinion and not a finding that earlier use violated a
term.

## Established versus unknown

**Established from current official sources:** the standard trial is purpose-
limited; public documentation expressly excludes business purposes that are
commercial or for-profit. A user may qualify for an Individual license as a
one-person/self-employed business for internal business needs, but that class
rule does not erase the separate Trial Purposes restriction. Enterprise and
Academic trials are separate paths.

**Not established from public pages or repository evidence:** the exact CLO
license class and account agreement attached to the owner's session; whether
any separately signed CLO agreement or written permission applies; and the
project's legal/business status or whether its product-development testing is
outside the excluded commercial/for-profit purposes. The owner described a
14-day trial and enabled features; neither fact resolves those conditions.

## Gate 0 disposition

Because the V03 use is product-development testing and the available evidence
does not affirmatively establish that this exact use falls within permitted
Trial Purposes or another applicable grant, the rights precondition is **not
cleared**. This does not assert that use is categorically unlawful or that a
past trial breached the terms. It means no further CLO fixtures or
import/export activity is authorized as V03 acceptance evidence on the
present record. Keep S475–S478 as bounded technical observations only; do not
claim CLO profile/conformance, general interoperability, or V03 completion.

To reopen CLO as a V03 candidate, the evidence must establish applicable
rights for this exact product-development use and the licensee/class (for
example, an applicable written grant or order, if one already exists), plus
the still-open local-processing and exact-profile gates. This slice does not
contact CLO or request additional account details. A rights-cleared alternate
receiver remains a separate path.
