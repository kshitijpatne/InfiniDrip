## Diagnosis — directly reproduced in current source

`curvedHemAndVent` computes `ventTop` by subtracting the selected depth from the hem-side endpoint. At zero depth, its `vent` line has identical start and end coordinates; the woven allowance assigns the named `vent` edge a 1 cm allowance. `outlineSamples` includes duplicate consecutive vertices at that degenerate edge, and the seam-allowance `normal` divides by the segment length without a zero-length guard. `seamAllowance` therefore produces non-finite cut coordinates on the front and back. `layoutPieces` propagates their non-finite bounds into all packed geometry and translated marks, which is why the complete 12-piece source DXF contains NaNs.

The committed diagnosis helper reproduces the current-source behavior: at `sideVentDepth=0`, the front and lower back each have a zero-length, 1 cm-allowance `vent` and two non-finite CUT coordinates; the `sideVentDepth=0.5 cm` supported control has nonzero vent edges and zero non-finite CUT coordinates. It makes no product-source edits. The appropriate source repair is to represent a closed vent as a continuous side/hem join with no zero-length vent edge or vent-top marker, while preserving the 0.5 cm and other nonzero vent constructions. This is proposed as S627 work, not included in S626.
