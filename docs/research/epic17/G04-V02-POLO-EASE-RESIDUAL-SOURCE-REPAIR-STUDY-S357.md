### Findings and disposition

- **Directly encoded source:** `src/drafting/sleeve.ts` declares `CAP_EASE = 1.5`; cap width is `m.bicep + m.ease * 0.5`; cap height is solved against `targetArmhole + CAP_EASE` by a 30-iteration binary search with lower bound 0. `src/drafting/polo.ts` passes the sum of its front and back armhole edge lengths into that sleeve component. `STANDARD_M` has bicep 38 cm and garment ease 10 cm, yielding 43 cm source sleeve width. The source stitch accepts measured ease in `[-1, 4]`, while the currently authored design result is +1.5 cm.
- **Fresh option basis:** the exact 17 S331 Polo option configurations were freshly drafted. Their armhole-changing topology is the same across the matrix: total front+back armhole length is 41.69145806677 cm and the authored total cap length is 43.19145806476 cm, +1.5 cm. The S351/S353 0.68 cm assembly residual is still present on all 34 live faces under the approved normalized-arclength display map; it remains above the explicit 0.25 cm gate.
- **In-memory matrix:** the source cap-curve formula and height solver were reproduced in a research-only harness. Ten candidate combinations (cap ease 0 or 1.5 cm × sleeve-width reductions 0, 0.5, 1, 1.308541933, or 1.5 cm) were evaluated across 17 configurations / 34 faces each: 170 rows / 340 face candidates. No production source was changed. With the original 43 cm width, lowering cap ease to zero still yields a 43 cm minimum cap, a 1.308541933 cm actual excess over the body armhole, and a 0.63 cm assembly residual on all 34 faces. At width reduction 1 cm, the residual remains 0.27 cm, above the 0.25 cm gate. With cap ease retained at 1.5 cm, every tested width reduction leaves at least one residual above threshold; across the 34 face candidates the maximum values range 0.64–0.74 cm. The research matrix is `S357-polo-ease-residual/ease-candidate-matrix.json`.
- **Concrete source-change candidate, not authorized:** a Polo-specific sleeve width reduction of 1.5 cm (43→41.5 cm), combined with cap ease 1.5→0 cm, yields 0 residual across all 34 candidate faces, exact cap/armhole lengths of 41.691458 cm, closed candidate sleeve-piece joins (0 cm), and the declared stitch check reports `-0.0 cm` within `[-1,4]`. It would reduce both `Sleeve bicep width` and `Sleeve hem` POMs by 1.5 cm; `Sleeve length (cap–hem)` changes by about −0.0313 cm in the standard matrix. This changes the explicitly authored ease, narrows the sleeve and cuff, and is not supported by any recorded physical sample or construction reference. It is a product/design decision, not a source-derived correction.
- **Output rejection:** the six same-revision candidate front/back renders (default, maximum stand height, maximum collar-leaf depth) are visibly watermarked diagnostic-only. The regular renderer rejects the existing sleeve colorway fill on every inspected candidate with `COLORWAY_CONTOUR_UNCLOSED` for `flat:polo:<face>:component:sleeve:outline`; only monochrome silhouette fallbacks were rendered for visual inspection. POM callouts were omitted in these diagnostic images. They therefore do not demonstrate a valid V02 output or clear any hold. No candidate is accepted.

Recommended: preserve the current pattern semantics and gate until a viable source-specific construction rule or explicit alternative acceptance contract is approved; the tested width/ease candidate is rejected because it changes POMs and still fails colorway closure. No source change or threshold waiver is implemented in S357.
