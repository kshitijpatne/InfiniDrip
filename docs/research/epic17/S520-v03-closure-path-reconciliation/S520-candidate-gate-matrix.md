## Unchanged exit gates

V03 still requires an identified, permitted receiver/build and documented
apparel exchange profile; acceptable local handling; clean or explicitly
understood import; source-to-output fidelity at `0.001 cm`; piece identity;
CUT/SEW/fold/notch/internal-mark semantics; native grade/size relationships;
negative-input rejection; and generation-two round trip across representative
fixtures for all seven recipes. Keep legacy DXF behavior and every protected
export identity unchanged. V02 all-seven acceptance is Done and is not reopened.

## Practical stop rule

No current candidate clears both permission and technical direction. Do not
restart CLO, re-open its dialogs, or repeat a receiver fixture to collect more
renders. The next decision-bearing operation is an authoritative CLO Support
answer on whether this exact local evaluation and its file handling fit the
trial grant, or written authorization/terms for a different receiver with
actual apparel-DXF bidirectional capability. Even a favorable CLO rights
answer would not cure its measured Polo geometry failure.
