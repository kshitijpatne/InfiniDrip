# Slice 344 — Woven sleeve-cap join diagnosis

## Evidence and conclusion

Reviewed the S343 production option matrix, current `WOVEN_SHIRT` draft source, revision-bound G04 projection map, rigid assembly placements and source-edge overlays generated from the same 28 option records. The generated evidence is diagnostic and does not release production output.

- All 28 canonical option records match the current Woven configuration matrix. The S343 Chromium replay reported 56/56 faces held and zero browser errors; the all-recipe criterion remains unchanged.
- **Directly encoded:** `Woven sleeve-cap ease` stitches `sleeve.capLeft + sleeve.capRight` to `front.armhole + yoke.armholeUpper + back.armholeLower`, in that source order. Its allowed ease is `[-1, +4] cm`. The current recipe drafts 46.10136 cm of cap against 44.60136 cm of body armhole in every option: `+1.50000 cm` ease. The authored stitch lengths and ease therefore establish a valid eased seam relationship; they do not define one rigid assembled contour.
- **Directly encoded:** `capLeft` runs underarm→crown and `capRight` crown→underarm. The front armhole runs shoulder→underarm; the yoke upper armhole runs shoulder→yoke/back join; the back lower armhole runs yoke/back join→underarm. The yoke/back seam endpoints agree exactly in the source. The body front and back totals are each approximately 22.30065 cm; the cap halves are approximately 23.05068 cm each. Band top and sleeve hem endpoints match to floating-point precision in both assembled views.
- **Approved view convention:** S321/S324 authorize the front/back cap segmentation by normalized arclength, reflected sleeve/band pairs, and the back-facing yoke role. This establishes which source interval belongs to each face; it does not specify deforming a displayed cap curve to remove the sewn ease.
- **Derived from all 28 drafts and current rigid placements:** the front underarm matches, but its shoulder/crown endpoints are 0.66144 cm apart in the diagnostic rigid placement. In the back, underarm matches; the shoulder/crown gap ranges by option, while the transferred sleeve station is 0.24222–0.30575 cm from the exact yoke/back join. Production assembly separately reports a 0.47 cm front residual on each face and a back solver conflict on each face; 26 backs also report 0.39 cm, the minimum-yoke-depth back has no numeric residual, and the maximum-yoke-depth back reports 0.73 cm. The 0.25 cm hold threshold remains unchanged.
- **Pattern-specific reference:** the Woven recipe record and stitch graph support the two-sleeve/band construction and the ordered ease relationship. No checked pattern-specific source establishes how an exterior flat should reshape or suppress the visible sleeve-cap edge when depicting that ease. No unchecked general reference is needed to locate these exact source joins.

The source topology is specified. The remaining conflict is the technical-flat convention for depicting a curved seam with 1.5 cm of authored ease while retaining readable joined front/back views. S343’s specific approval is limited to drawing the body neckline once and suppressing its duplicate stand-base stroke; that approval does not decide the sleeve-cap display.

The yoke's `armholeUpper` boundary is emitted as part of the yoke outline on `GARMENT_SILHOUETTE` in all 28 generated back geometries, not as a separately authored `SEAM` primitive. This report records the actual layer; no seam-layer change is made in S344. S321 approved the yoke's back-facing view placement, and the exact requested presentation layer should be checked explicitly in the next implementation boundary.

The default and yoke-depth-maximum overlays (`woven-shirt-default-front/back.png` and `woven-shirt-yokeDepth-max-front/back.png`) were inspected. They show the source body and sleeve curves after current rigid placement, not a finished technical flat. The front curves diverge at the shoulder/crown. The default back curves cross/diverge around the upper cap; the mapped yoke/back station is offset even though the source yoke/back endpoints coincide. These overlays are not evidence that a warped display would pass crossings, overlap, fill or readability review.

## Concrete proposal for maintainer decision

**Proposed view-only eased seam projection (not implemented):** preserve the pattern revision, piece coordinates, cut outlines, stitch arrays and `[-1,+4] cm` source ease. For each face, map the sleeve cap interval by normalized arclength onto the corresponding same-revision body join and render one shared visible interface. Keep two reflected sleeve and band instances. On the front, map `sleeve.capLeft` (underarm→crown) onto `front.armhole` in reverse traversal (underarm→shoulder). On the back, map `sleeve.capRight` (crown→underarm) onto the concatenated `yoke.armholeUpper` then `back.armholeLower` path in forward traversal (yoke shoulder→yoke/back seam→underarm). Put the station between those two back edges at the exact `yoke.armholeUpper` arclength fraction of their combined length. Keep the existing yoke/back seam join and the exact sleeve-band top/hem join. Draw the shared body-derived seam once on `SEAM`, suppress the duplicate sleeve-cap stroke, and retain hidden colorway boundaries only where required. Keep the sleeve/yoke pieces on their currently approved front/back faces and draw the yoke boundary at its correct internal seam layer.

**Basis and effect:** source piece IDs, stitch order, endpoint directions, the 1.5 cm ease and body yoke/back endpoint agreement are directly encoded. The proposed curve transfer is a design convention, not construction evidence or a physical claim. It would make the rendered cap interface coincide with the body seam and remove the displayed 1.5 cm ease; the cut pattern and stitch graph remain unchanged. It would therefore change the apparent cap curvature and must be labeled/implemented as view geometry. Assembly validation must verify the exact same-revision interfaces and authored ease range without changing the generic 0.25 cm gate for unrelated joins. If the convention is declined, all 56 Woven faces remain held. If approved, it still does not pass any face until the full 28-configuration production output has no joins, crossings, overlap, double strokes or unreadable POM/callouts.

**Recommendation:** approve this bounded view-only projection for the Woven sleeve-cap seam, subject to the all-28 output checks above. Its practical consequence is a coherent technical-flat seam depiction while preserving the source pattern and the recorded ease value. A non-warped rendering or the current rigid depiction would retain the specific offsets/solver conflicts and keep Woven held.
