## Findings verified by Codex

- **GRAFIS:** The free training version supports Windows 10/11 but the official
  page says pattern output and import/export are disabled. The same license
  limits it to private training/test use and excludes commercial/freelancer
  activity. The official commercial-system help separately documents AAMA/DXF
  and ASTM/DXF formats; its sales FAQ says the exchange module is not included
  in every standard package and may require separate purchase. The official
  price page lists annual and three-month paid licenses. So the free edition
  cannot serve as a V03 receiver, while full exchange access has no qualifying
  no-cost route in the sources reviewed.
- **Gerber AccuMark:** Official help documents import of AAMA/ASTM apparel data
  and export of models in ASTM/AAMA DXF. Lectra's current product page lists
  AccuMark 2026.1; the detailed help pages are not tied to that build. The
  official configuration page scopes Windows 11 support to V15+ and
  64-bit Enterprise/Professional editions, with Home/Consumer unsupported.
  Official licensing/download help requires assigned product access and
  account/license validation; Lectra publishes an annual-subscription route.
  The reviewed pages do not document a free trial or free license, strict
  local-only exchange processing, same-receiver fidelity, graded-fixture
  fidelity, or malformed-input behavior.
- Neither candidate produces a witnessed InfiniDrip fixture round-trip or
  qualifies as the no-cost, rights-cleared, local receiver required for V03.

## Codex wording correction

OpenCode labels the no-cost-access dimension “FAIL” for AccuMark while its
description says no no-cost route was documented. Missing public proof does
not prove that no exception can exist, so the Codex matrix records this as
“not established / not qualified on the current evidence,” not as a factual
claim that no free route is possible. The user preference still means no
purchase, download, or installation is pursued.
