## Candidate decision

Do not select the Free plan as G04's receiver. It fails two independent
published checks: it does not list DXF export, and its free-use terms do not
authorize organization/commercial product-development use. The desktop app's
general DXF menus and Gerber/Lectra statement establish technical potential
for some paid/authorized configurations only; they do not override the Free
plan's permission table or prove exact profile behavior. Rights, local-only
file handling, exact profile semantics, and round-trip behavior remain
uncleared. No account, file, or software action occurred.
