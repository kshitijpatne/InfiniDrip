## S610 decision and next step

The identical set of five one-vertex-short CUT roles appears in the S536 AAMA
and S609 ASTM receiver outputs. Because input encoding and output format both
changed between those experiments, their individual effects are not isolated.
The CLO optimization controls that might delete/alter points were disabled in
S609; official help does not identify this loss mechanism or promise the
required tolerance. The stage and exact cause remain unknown. Do not spend
another slice toggling the same options without a source-backed prediction.

All V03 holds remain. Research a distinct free/local apparel CAD receiver or
documented profile with rights and an evidence-backed practical test path. If
none is available, bring forward that specific blocker; do not relax the
all-seven criterion or geometry threshold.
