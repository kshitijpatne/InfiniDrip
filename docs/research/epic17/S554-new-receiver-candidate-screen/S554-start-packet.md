### Optitex PDS — promising capability candidate, not qualified

- The current official Optitex site describes an interoperable 2D/3D pattern-design product and links the Help Center, but the Help Center now redirects the exact import/export/system-requirements pages to a customer login. Official search-index snippets on those pages advertise DXF/AAMA/ASTM export and AAMA/ASTM/DXF import; the ASTM version/edition is not given in accessible public material. A grading RUL sidecar is mentioned in the snippets, but complete grade, notch, drill, internal-line and CUT/SEW semantics are not available for inspection.
- The public EULA grants a limited license for uses specified in product documentation and for business use; it does not establish that an evaluation trial exists or that product-development evaluation is included in any trial. It also says the software may transmit technical data about use to Optitex systems. This is not evidence that DXF contents are uploaded, and is not a local-only processing guarantee. Hosted solutions have separate terms stating hosted data resides on provider servers; that cloud service is not the desktop receiver under consideration.
- Result: do not install or transfer fixtures. Exact profile version, evaluation rights, file-processing locality, and mark/semantic behavior remain unsupported by inspectable public primary evidence. If its customer portal becomes available under an already-authorized account, the bounded next evidence is the current PDS manual/profile edition, applicable evaluation/order terms, and a desktop-only mode contract; otherwise do not contact the supplier or infer permission.
- Official sources: [Optitex PDS export](https://help.optitex.com/1382687/Content/Optitex_2D/Export_To_CAD_CAM_Files.htm) and [import guidance](https://help.optitex.com/1382687/Content/Optitex_2D/Import_best_practices_for_standard_formats.htm) (both currently login-gated); [Optitex EULA](https://optitex.com/eula/); [Optitex OPTINEDCloud agreement](https://optitex.com/optinedcloud-gtc/); [Optitex product site](https://optitex.com/).
