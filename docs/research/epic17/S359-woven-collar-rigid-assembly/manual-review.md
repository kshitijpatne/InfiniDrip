No source ease/deformation rule supplies a nonlinear assembled-view transform.

This reproduces the source-assembly residual range; it is not a new allowance or a threshold change.

## Remaining mapping proposal — requires explicit design approval

- **Proposed source-to-view relation:** apply a revision-bound, view-only, piecewise normalized-arclength projection to the full Woven collar stack, not just the already-approved base seam stroke. Map `outerStand.neckline`'s authored CF-to-CB station coordinate and the corresponding `innerStand`, `upperCollar`, and `underCollar` stitched stations to the same cumulative `front.neckline` / reversed `yoke.neckline` receiving curve. On each source role, preserve the signed local normal distance from its exact sewn base edge and map that offset along the receiver curve's local normal. Use the existing shoulder station split: FRONT `front.centerFront.end`↔`front.shoulder.start`; BACK `yoke.centerBack.end`↔`yoke.shoulder.start`. Keep the source `outerStand.neckline` suppressed in the rendered seam layer under the already-approved S343 base-seam display; show the body neckline once as the receiver seam. Keep current source IDs, revision binding, approved collar exterior arclength stations, and existing fold-reflected view instances/multiplicity. Preserve existing SVG ordering: silhouette, artwork, seam, construction/details, then closure/topstitch/callout/annotation; keep collar silhouettes in their current silhouette layer and the single body seam in `SEAM`.
- **What supports it:** exact source order and edge lengths establish a monotone, piecewise coordinate correspondence and shoulder break; the S321 exterior mapping already records the same CB-to-CF normalized station convention. The curves and current overlays establish why rigid placement cannot work. Those facts support the coordinates only; they do not encode a deformed display convention.
- **Assumptions and effect:** normal offsets are assumed to preserve the drawn stand/collar thickness and order while the base follows the receiver curve. This bends the displayed collar/stand contours and can alter apparent exterior silhouette, collisions, and callout positions; it does not modify cut geometry, source stitch graph, or generic DXF. It is not evidence of physical turn-of-cloth, easing distribution, fit, or factory acceptance. Implementation would require a separate slice and the full 28-option/56-face checks for joins, contour crossings, overlap/layers, colorway closure, exact POM anchors, and readable output. If not approved, keep all 56 Woven faces held.
- **Recommendation:** keep the complete Woven hold until a candidate projection is explicitly approved and passes its full output matrix. This is an output convention decision, not something the pattern or checked construction references settle.
