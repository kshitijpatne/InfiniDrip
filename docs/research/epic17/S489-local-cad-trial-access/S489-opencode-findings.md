# S489 — OpenCode independent findings: StyleCAD and Optitex access

Review date: 2026-10-08. Official publisher sources only. No download,
installation, account, trial acceptance, vendor contact, payment, fixture,
app/browser launch, file processing, or upload occurred.

## StyleCAD

SoftFashion's product pages present StyleCAD as desktop apparel CAD. Its
pricing page lists the PE and Split PE/PG systems with generic DXF import and
export and directs buyers to request a quote. The Pattern Expert page offers
a vendor-arranged online demo; the reviewed pages do not say this provides a
downloadable local build or an evaluation license. Its FAQ documents machine-
bound license files, hardware-key activation, and dealer-issued module
activation. This identifies a gated local-use path but does not prove that an
unpublished free exception cannot exist.

The StyleCAD FAQ documents AAMA DXF import. The pricing page's generic DXF
import/export listing does not establish AAMA/ASTM export semantics or
round-trip fidelity. No exact build, no-cost local grant, or export profile is
established by the reviewed public sources. Conclusion: StyleCAD does not
clear V03 Gate 0 on current evidence; the online demo cannot be treated as a
local receiver license.

## Optitex

Optitex separates installed PDS from the hosted OPTINEDCloud service. The
current customer portal lists the O/26.1 PDS installer and free viewers, but
states that software installation needs a valid license key and directs users
without one to Sales. The software EULA ties activation access to purchase
documentation/license-key provisioning. The free-trial clauses found apply
to OPTINEDCloud Subscription Services, which the service agreement defines as
internet-based software that can upload, share, view, and process apparel
files. That is not a local PDS trial and does not meet G04's local-only
receiver boundary.

Publisher support and bundle pages document AAMA/DXF import into Optitex and
standard-format import capability, but the reviewed pages do not establish
the exact AAMA/ASTM export profile for a no-cost build. No same-receiver
round-trip, grading fixture, or invalid-input evidence exists. Conclusion:
Optitex does not clear V03 Gate 0 on current evidence. This is not a claim
that no unpublished exception could exist.

## Decision and limits

Neither candidate has a documented rights-cleared, no-cost Windows local
receiver route. StyleCAD's demo is not a local license grant; Optitex's
explicit free trial is hosted, while the local PDS installation is license
key-gated. Preserve every V03 rights, local-handling, exact-profile,
round-trip, grading, and negative-input gate. Exact trial-registration terms,
gated help pages, dealer/Sales responses, exact AAMA/ASTM export mapping, and
local-only processing remain unknown. No legal conclusion is made.
