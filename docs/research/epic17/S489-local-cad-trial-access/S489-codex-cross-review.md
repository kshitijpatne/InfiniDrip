# S489 — Codex cross-review

Codex independently reopened the publisher product, pricing, trial, EULA,
support, and download pages after OpenCode returned `WORK FINISHED`. The result
and limitations below reflect the current public evidence reviewed
2026-10-08.

## Findings

- **StyleCAD:** PDS Pattern Expert's vendor-arranged online demo does not
  describe a downloadable local evaluation build or grant. The pricing page
  puts desktop PE systems with DXF import/export behind a quote request. Its
  FAQ documents license files, hardware keys, dealer activation, and AAMA DXF
  import. Those sources do not establish no-cost local rights or an AAMA/ASTM
  export profile. Keep both unknowns explicit; do not treat the demo as a local
  license or infer export semantics from import support.
- **Optitex:** The O/26.1 portal lists an installed PDS package separately from
  its free viewer and says a valid license key is needed for software
  installation. The software EULA ties activation to purchase documentation
  and a license key. The explicit free-trial terms reviewed govern
  OPTINEDCloud, which is defined as an internet SaaS that can upload/share/view/
  process apparel files. That hosted offer is not a local PDS trial and cannot
  satisfy G04's local receiver gate. PDS bundles/support document standard
  import (including AAMA/ASTM/DXF), but the reviewed pages do not establish an
  exact no-cost AAMA/ASTM export profile, same-receiver round trip, grading
  preservation, or malformed-input behavior.

## Corrections and limits

`getstylecad.com` presents an inquiry-based subscription under the StyleCAD
name, but Codex does not rely on it as proof of a specific license grant or
terms. Public pages do not prove that no private exception or trial exists;
they do not document one. The exact terms shown after a trial registration,
gated help pages, and any user-specific license grants remain unreviewed. No
legal conclusion is made.

Neither candidate qualifies on present evidence. Preserve the V03 rights,
local-only handling, exact build/profile, actual round-trip, grading, and
negative-input gates. No installation, account, trial acceptance, vendor
contact, file transfer, or product change occurred.
